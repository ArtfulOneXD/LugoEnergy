Lighting FAQs

Can you add recessed lights to an existing room?

Yes. We lay out spacing, cut carefully, and fish wiring to keep ceilings clean while meeting clearance rules.

Do you install chandeliers in high ceilings?

Yes. We use proper lifts, bracing, and alignment for tall entries and stairwells.

Can you replace old fluorescent fixtures with LEDs?

Yes. We remove ballasts as needed, install LED fixtures, and ensure switching and dimming work smoothly.

Do you install smart switches and dimmers?

Yes. We match dimmers to your LED fixtures to avoid flicker and buzzing, and set up 3-way controls where helpful.
