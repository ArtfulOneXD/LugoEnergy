About LugoEnergy

LugoEnergy is a Sacramento-based residential electrician focused on clean, safe, and code-compliant work. We help homeowners with everything from panel upgrades and EV charger installations to lighting, outlet repairs, and troubleshooting stubborn electrical issues.

Because we work in Sacramento homes every week, we understand the quirks of older panels, mixed wiring, and remodels. We label panels clearly, keep work areas clean, and explain options before we start. Common projects include upgrading capacity for EV charging, adding dedicated circuits for remodels, and fixing recurring breaker trips.

We primarily serve Sacramento, Elk Grove, Rocklin, Folsom, Roseville, and surrounding neighborhoods, with flexible scheduling and honest communication on every job. As a licensed and insured C-10 contractor, we prioritize safety, neat workmanship, and transparent pricing.
