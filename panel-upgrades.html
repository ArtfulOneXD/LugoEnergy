Panel Upgrade FAQs

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger or solar?

Often yes. We calculate available load and add capacity or subpanels so EV chargers and future solar interconnections meet code.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most residential upgrades are completed the same day once power is disconnected, with inspection shortly after.

Do you handle permits and utility coordination?

Yes. We pull permits, schedule utility shutdowns if required, and stay through inspection.

Can you add surge protection and labeling?

Yes. We add whole-home surge protection on request and fully label all breakers for future service.
